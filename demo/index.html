How the clock works

A ticket created at 17:50 on Friday with a 30-minute urgent target is due at 08:20 on Monday, not at 18:20 on Friday. Holidays are closed days. Time in Pending customer is subtracted from both elapsed time and the pause count.

remaining = target − businessMinutes(createdAt → now, open hours) + customerPause
